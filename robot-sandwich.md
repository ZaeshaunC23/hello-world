# hello-world
My first repository on GitHub

Assumptions: One clean plate is available, one butter knife is available, one jar of peanut butter is available and contains peanut butter, one jar of jelly is available and contains jelly, one loaf of bread is available and contains at least two slices, the sandwich will be assembled on the plate.


Place the plate on a flat surface
pick up the loaf of bread
Open the bread package.
remove one slice of bread from the package
Remove another slice of bread from the package.
Place the second slice of bread next to the first slice of bread on the plate
close the bread package
pick up the peanut butter jar
unscrew the lid from the peanut butter jar
place the lid on a flat surface
pick up the butter knife
insert the blade of the knife through the peanut butter until peanut butter is attached to the blade
remove the knife from the peanut butter jar
move the knife above the first slice of bread
touch the peanut butter to the top surface of the first slice of bread.
move the knife back and fourth until the top surface of the first slice is covered with peanut butter.
Insert the knife the into the peanut butter jar again if additional peanut butter is needed.
Remove the knife from the peanut butter jar.
Spread the additional peanut butter until the first slice is covered
place the knife on the plate
pick up the peanut butter lid
screw the lid onto the peanut butter jar 
pick up the jelly jar
unscrew lid from the jelly jar 
place the lid on the flat surface
pick up the knife
insert the blade of the knife into the jelly
move the knife through the jelly until jelly is attached to the blade
remove the knife from the jelly jar
move the knife above the second slice of bread
touch the jelly to the top surface of the second slice of bread
move the knife back and forth until the top surface of the second slice is covered in jelly
insert the knife into the jelly jar again if additional jelly is needed
remove the knife from the jelly jar
spread additional jelly until the second slice is covered
place the knife on the plate
pick up the jelly jar lid 
screw the lid onto the jelly jar
pick up the slice of bread covered with peanut butter 
turn the peanut butter covered side downward
place the peanut butter covered side on top of the jelly covered side of the slice of bread
place the sandwich on the plate
